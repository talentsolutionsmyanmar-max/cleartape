Check the tape before you ape.

ClearTape is a public beginner desk. It exists so people stop buying a ticker because a promotional account posted it. Most early calls are late ads. Most new tokens die from rugs, honeypots, insider dumps, or paid amplification — not from the market.

First-pass desk

Paste a Solana mint or an EVM contract. The desk reads public DexScreener data and, when possible, RugCheck. It does not connect your wallet and it does not store addresses.

Always copy the address from an explorer or the project’s own site. Never from a DM.

Instant fails

Any one of these is enough to walk away.

ABORT

Mint authority still live

If mint authority is not revoked, the deployer can print infinite supply. Instant skip.

ABORT

Freeze / blacklist / honeypot sell lock

You can buy and never sell. Run Honeypot.is on EVM.

ABORT

Liquidity unlocked and in one wallet

Want burned LP or a lock you can verify on a known locker — not a screenshot.

ABORT

Unverified contract

Treat unverified source as hostile.

ABORT

Contract arrived by DM

Official site then explorer then scanners. Never the other way around.

STOP

Top holder cluster over ~20%

Bundled launch wallets are the modern soft rug.

STOP

Deployer has a graveyard

If nine of ten prior tokens are dead, this is a job, not a project.

STOP

Paid caller with no personal tape

Research the token as if the tweet did not exist.

Callers are not oracles

A promotional amplifier is advertising, not research.

REAL

A call you can audit

Tweet exists before the move. Wallet is public. Losing calls stay up.

FAKE

A promo dressed as alpha

Deleted misses, cropped PnL, VIP funnel.

Caller tape test

  1. Is the oldest public mention actually early?
  2. Did they buy, or only post?
  3. Are losers still on the timeline?
  4. Are they paid / launching their own ticker now?
