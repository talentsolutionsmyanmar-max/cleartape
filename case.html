The $DRAFT-class mistake.

Someone asked for the real account with legit early calls. The account they were looking at was a promotional amplifier. The ticker it pushed has to be researched as a promo object, not as someone’s verified win.

What the tape actually said

TAPE

No standout original call history

Public posts do not show a stack of dated early mentions that later became obvious winners.

TAPE

Style is amplifier / curator

Boosts other callers and adds an occasional new shill. That is distribution.

TAPE

Older era does not match the pitch

Older activity looked NFT-project related around 2022, then the feed shifted to meme-coin calling.

TAPE

The ticker was the product

$DRAFT-class cashtags inherit no trust from the caption. Scan the mint as if the tweet never existed.

Promotional account. Not a proven personal call book.

Do not follow the handle into a buy. Run the contract through the first-pass desk, then official rug tools.

Do this in ten minutes

  1. Ignore the caption. Copy the contract from an explorer or official site only.
  2. Run the scanner on the first-pass desk.
  3. Open official tools the desk prints.
  4. Audit the handle with the same ticker.
  5. If any instant fail prints, walk.
