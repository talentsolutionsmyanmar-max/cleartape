Open the tape

Four questions. Fail one, ignore the call.

  1. Was the first public mention actually early? Open Latest. Scroll to the first cashtag post. If price had already run 3x–10x, they are late liquidity.
  2. Did they buy, or only post? A cropped PnL is not a fill.
  3. Are losers still on the timeline? Deleted misses means marketer.
  4. Are they paid, or launching their own bag? VIP TG or own token means distribution, not your PnL.
